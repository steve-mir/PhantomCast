Pricing FAQ

Why Bitcoin only?

We process payments through NOWPayments, which is non-custodial — your payment goes directly into our wallet without an intermediary holding it. No bank, no card-network fees, no chargeback fraud, and works in jurisdictions traditional payment rails don't reach. Lightning support is on the roadmap.

Why a $770 lifetime instead of a subscription?

The desktop app does not get more expensive for us to operate as you use it more. Charging recurring rent on local software didn't sit right. The $30/mo Premium add-on covers the live + studio features whose backend infra (license tracking, OBS plugin updates, batch hooks) does grow with usage.

What does "lifetime" mean exactly?

All Phantom Cast 0.x updates, forever. If we ship a 1.x with a major rebuild we may offer an upgrade path at a discounted rate; existing 0.x continues to work and continues to receive security patches.

Can I activate Phantom Cast on more than one machine?

Yes — the Lifetime License covers 2 simultaneous device activations. If you need more, deactivate one from the dashboard or contact us for a multi-seat license. Moving to a new machine is a one-click flow and rate-limited to once per year to discourage license sharing.

What if I just want Premium for one month?

Buy a single month — no auto-renewal unless you opt in. You can also batch-buy 12 months at the discounted annual rate. Premium attaches to your existing license key and unlocks immediately on the next app launch (or within 6 hours of revocation if you don't renew).

Refund policy?

14 days, no questions. Refund is paid back in BTC to the address you specify. Full refund policy.
