How activation works

  1. 1
    Pay in Bitcoin

    Pick a tier, enter your email, and pay the $910 BTC invoice on the next screen. Funds settle directly to a Phantom Cast wallet — non-custodial.

  2. 2
    Receive your activation key

    Your PC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX key appears on the page as soon as the payment confirms on-chain.

  3. 3
    Activate inside the app

    Open Phantom Cast → Activate license → paste the key. You get 30 days of full feature access immediately.

  4. 4
    Continue with a monthly subscription

    After day 30 your license rolls over to your tier's monthly subscription. Manage or cancel anytime from your billing dashboard.

Common questions

What does the $910 cover?

The activation key, plus 30 days of full feature access on the tier you chose. After day 30 you continue at the tier's monthly rate ($19 Pro / $49 Studio), or downgrade to free.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes — switch any time from Billing in your dashboard. Tier changes take effect at the next monthly cycle.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime — no questions, no retention calls. Your activation key still works on the free tier with the watermark and 1080p cap.

Is there a refund?

14-day no-questions refund — refunded in BTC to a wallet you specify. See the refund policy.

I already have an activation key — how do I subscribe to a plan?

If you've already activated and your free 30 days are running out, head to the subscription page instead.
