Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Phantom Cast is a powerful tool. We don't pretend it can't be misused. This policy is what we will enforce, what we expect you to enforce on yourself, and what we will report to law enforcement when relevant.

Prohibited uses

You may not use Phantom Cast — or any output produced with it — to:

  1. Create non-consensual likenesses of real people. If the subject of either the source or target identity is a real person who has not given informed consent for that likeness to be used in your output, you may not produce it.
  2. Impersonate a real person for fraud, scam, harassment, defamation, or to deceive a counterparty. This includes "sextortion" material, fake KYC submissions, and synthetic-evidence fabrication.
  3. Produce political deepfakes, including but not limited to: fabricated statements attributed to candidates, election officials, or public figures during election periods; synthetic content designed to influence voting.
  4. Produce sexual content depicting any individual under 18, including likenesses that resemble a minor. This is an instant tier-1 ban and is reported to the relevant authorities (NCMEC in the US, IWF in the UK, and equivalent national bodies elsewhere).
  5. Produce sexual content depicting any real person without their explicit, informed, written consent — even where local law permits.
  6. Distribute synthetic content without disclosing its synthetic nature where required by law in your jurisdiction (EU AI Act, US state-level synthetic-media disclosure laws, etc.).
  7. Bypass content moderation systems on third-party platforms (e.g., to defeat KYC, identity verification, or "real human" gating).
  8. Reverse engineer, redistribute, or repackage the Phantom Cast software or its bundled models.

Consent — what counts

Consent must be: informed (the subject knows specifically how their likeness will be used), specific (covers this use case, not "any use"), recent (not from a wholly different era of their life), and revocable. Consent for one project does not transfer to another.

Enforcement

  • We act on credible reports sent to abuse@phantomcast.app.
  • For verified violations of Section 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8: we revoke the license; revocation propagates within 6 hours via license heartbeat. No refund.
  • For Section 4 (any sexual content involving minors): instant ban, license revoked, the customer record is preserved for the legally required retention period, and a referral is made to the appropriate authority.
  • For Section 5: license revoked. Subject of the content may also pursue claims against you directly under their local right-of-publicity, image rights, or harassment statutes.

Appeals

If you believe a revocation was issued in error, email appeals@phantomcast.app with your order ID and a written response to the abuse report. Appeals are reviewed by a human within 10 business days.

Reporting misuse

If you've been harmed by Phantom Cast misuse, or have evidence of any of the above, please email abuse@phantomcast.app with as much detail as possible. We will respond within 24 hours and investigate within 5 business days.
