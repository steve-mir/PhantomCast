Architecture

Privacy & data — exactly what we collect

This is a non-legal companion to the Privacy Policy. Where the policy answers "what we do with what we collect", this page answers "what we collect, full stop, with the architectural rationale".

Frames, audio, video — never transmitted

Inference runs locally on your GPU. Phantom Cast does not have an upload pipeline. There is no server-side rendering. There is no "send for cloud enhancement" toggle. The bytes never leave your machine — verifiable by inspecting outbound network traffic with Wireshark, Little Snitch, or your firewall of choice.

What we deliberately don't collect

  • Your raw video/audio/webcam frames
  • Source or target identity images
  • Project files, render output, file paths
  • Browsing history, other apps, system processes
  • Raw machine identifiers (volume serial, MAC, etc.) — only the SHA-256 composite hash leaves the device

Optional offline mode

Once activated, you can flip Settings → Privacy → Offline mode to disable every network call for up to 30 days between heartbeats. The app continues to work fully during the offline window. Reconnect any time within 30 days to refresh.

Telemetry & analytics

The desktop app does not ship with crash analytics or product telemetry by default. The website uses self-hosted PostHog (EU region) for product analytics; you can opt out via the cookie banner.

Data subject requests (DSAR)

If you're an EU/UK/CA resident, you can request a data export or full deletion at privacy@phantomcast.space. We fulfill within 30 days as required by GDPR. The export is a JSON bundle of every record tied to your email + license.

For the legal-grade version see the Privacy Policy. For B2B customers needing a DPA, see DPA.
