Refund Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

We want you to use Phantom Cast and feel like you got more value than you paid. If you don't, here's the deal.

14-day, no-questions refund

Within 14 calendar days of purchase, you can request a full refund without giving a reason. To start the refund, email billing@phantomcast.app with:

  • Your order ID (in the receipt email)
  • A Bitcoin (BTC) address to send the refund to

How BTC refunds work

The refund is paid back in BTC. We refund the same USD amount you originally paid, converted at the BTC/USD rate at the time of refund (not the time of purchase). Bitcoin price moves — be aware of this:

  • If BTC has risen since you paid, you'll receive less BTC than you sent us.
  • If BTC has fallen since you paid, you'll receive more BTC than you sent us.
  • Either way, the dollar value at the moment of refund matches what you paid.

Most refunds settle within 24 hours of approval. The on-chain network fee is paid by us.

What's eligible

  • Lifetime License ($770) — fully refundable within 14 days of purchase.
  • Premium add-on monthly ($30) — fully refundable within 14 days of that month's purchase, prorated by unused days if used.

What's not eligible

  • Purchases older than 14 days.
  • Licenses that have been revoked under the Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Refunds requested in fiat (USD/EUR/GBP via bank transfer). We pay refunds in BTC because that's how we received the payment.

Statutory consumer rights (EU/UK)

If you're a consumer resident in the EU or UK, you also benefit from the statutory right to withdraw within 14 days under the Consumer Contracts Regulations / Consumer Rights Directive. Our 14-day no-questions policy meets or exceeds those statutory rights. Note that by downloading and activating the desktop software you agree the supply begins immediately, which under EU/UK law would normally waive the statutory withdrawal right — we voluntarily honor it anyway.

Disputes

If a refund is denied and you disagree, email appeals@phantomcast.app with the order ID and the original denial. Appeals are reviewed by a human within 10 business days.
