Frequently asked

My GPU is supported but the app says it's running on CPU — what's wrong?

Open Settings → GPU diagnostics in the app. It walks through CUDA toolkit, NVIDIA driver, cuDNN, and TensorRT detection step-by-step and tells you exactly which one failed. Most often: driver version < 525.60 (update from nvidia.com/drivers). See the full GPU troubleshooting guide.

How do I route Phantom Cast into Twitch / YouTube Live / Zoom?

Premium add-on enables OBS virtual camera output. Once installed, pick "Phantom Cast Virtual Camera" as the video source in OBS, Zoom, Meet, or Discord. Full walkthrough in OBS integration.

I bought the license — where's my key?

Once your BTC payment confirms (typically 10–30 min), the key is emailed via Resend and shown in your dashboard. If 60 min after the on-chain confirmation no key has arrived, contact support — we can verify the order ID against the BTC txid manually.

How do I move my license to a new computer?

From the desktop app: Settings → License → Move. Or in the dashboard: Devices → Deactivate on the old machine. Limited to one move per year to discourage license sharing — contact support if you need an extra move (we honor genuine cases like a stolen laptop).

Can I use Phantom Cast offline?

Yes. After the first activation, you can enable offline mode in settings. The license heartbeat is then deferred for up to 30 days; the app continues to work fully during that window. Reconnect any time within 30 days to refresh.

Do you have an Apple Silicon build?

In private beta as of May 2026 — request access via contact. Linux is targeted for H2 2026.

My BTC payment confirmed but says "expired" — what now?

Don't worry — NOWPayments will still credit late confirmations. If the dashboard hasn't updated within 6 hours of on-chain confirmation, email billing@phantomcast.space with the order ID and the BTC txid; we'll resolve manually.

Is there a refund policy?

14 days, no questions. Refunded to a BTC address you specify. Full policy.

Are my videos used to train your models?

No. We don't have your videos. Inference is local; nothing is uploaded. We also don't train on customer footage even when shared voluntarily — see the privacy policy.

How do I report someone misusing Phantom Cast?

Email abuse@phantomcast.space with as much detail as possible. We act on verified reports within 6 hours by revoking the offending license, and cooperate with law enforcement on criminal cases.
