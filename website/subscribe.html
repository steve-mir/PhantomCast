Common questions

Do I need an activation key to subscribe?

Yes. The activation key binds the license to your hardware. Buy a key here — it includes the first 30 days of full access before any subscription starts.

When does my subscription start?

After the 30-day full-access window from your activation. You're prompted to subscribe a few days before it expires; you can also subscribe earlier from your dashboard.

How do I switch between Pro and Studio?

From Billing in your dashboard. Changes apply at the next monthly cycle; we don't charge mid-month upgrade fees.

What happens if I cancel?

Your tier features stay active until the end of your paid month, then drop to free (1080p, watermarked). Re-subscribe any time — no re-activation needed.
