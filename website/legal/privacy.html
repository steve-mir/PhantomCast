Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

What we do not collect

  • Your video, audio, or webcam frames — they never leave your machine
  • Your source/target identity images, project files, or render outputs
  • Raw machine identifiers (volume serial, MAC, GPU UUID); only the SHA-256 composite hash leaves the device
  • Browsing history, other applications, or any system-wide telemetry

Sub-processors

  • NOWPayments (payment processing) — non-custodial; receives the BTC amount, order ID, and customer email
  • Resend (transactional email) — receives email address and the email body content
  • Cloudflare (CDN, DNS) — receives IP and request metadata for static assets
  • Google Cloud Firestore (Frankfurt region, EU) — stores the data listed above

International transfers

Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Sub-processors located outside the EU operate under Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Your rights (EU/UK)

Under GDPR / UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to the processing of your data. To exercise any of these, email privacy@phantomcast.space. We respond within 30 days.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not "sell" personal information as defined under CCPA. The "Do not sell or share my personal information" link in our footer is provided as a courtesy. California residents may request access or deletion via the email above.

Cookies

The marketing site uses one essential session cookie (cart state) and, if you consent, a single analytics cookie (PostHog, EU). The customer portal uses an authentication cookie (Firebase Auth, essential).

Changes

We'll notify you of material changes by email at least 30 days before they take effect.
