Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

For Phantom Cast Ltd. multi-seat / studio customers · v1.0 · May 2026

This DPA forms part of the Terms of Service between Phantom Cast Ltd. ("Processor") and the entity that has signed up for a multi-seat or studio license ("Controller"). It applies where Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of Controller in the course of providing the Services.

1. Roles & processing

Controller determines the purposes and means of processing. Processor processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller, including with regard to international transfers.

2. Categories of data subjects & data

  • Subjects: Controller's authorized users (typically employees / contractors) of the Phantom Cast software.
  • Data: business email, name, license fingerprint hash (composite SHA-256), IP (hashed), heartbeat timestamps, support ticket content. No customer media (frames, audio, video, project files) is processed by Processor.

3. Sub-processors

Processor uses the sub-processors listed in our Privacy Policy. Controller authorizes these in advance. Processor will give 30 days' notice of any new sub-processor and Controller may object with reasonable cause.

4. Security

Processor implements appropriate technical and organizational measures including: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256); least-privilege access controls and audit logging on the Firestore project; an incident response plan with 72-hour breach notification commitment; periodic third-party security review.

5. International transfers

Personal Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Where transfers outside the EEA / UK occur via sub-processors, the Standard Contractual Clauses (EU 2021/914) apply, with the UK Addendum and Swiss Addendum as relevant.

6. Data subject rights

Processor will assist Controller in responding to data subject access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, and objection requests within the timelines required by GDPR.

7. Audit

On reasonable notice and no more than once per 12 months, Controller may audit Processor's compliance with this DPA. SOC 2 Type II reports (when available) and recent penetration test summaries can be shared under NDA in lieu of on-site audit where appropriate.

8. Return / deletion

On termination of the Services, Processor will delete or return all Personal Data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., financial records under tax law).

9. Liability

The liability terms in the Terms of Service apply to this DPA.

10. Signing

For B2B customers requiring a counter-signed DPA, email legal@phantomcast.space with your company name and we will return a counter-signed PDF within 5 business days.
