Boilerplate (100 words)

Phantom Cast is GPU-accelerated face-swap software that runs entirely on the user's local machine. Designed for streamers, content creators, and indie filmmakers, it delivers 30 fps real-time webcam swapping and 4K video export with GPEN-512 face enhancement — without uploading a single frame to the cloud. Phantom Cast is sold as a $770 one-time lifetime license, with an optional $30/month Premium add-on unlocking live streaming output (OBS virtual camera), batch processing, and Hyperswap full-head mode. The company was founded in 2025 and is funded entirely by its founders, with offices in Berlin, Lisbon, and Toronto.

Brand do's and don'ts

Do
  • • Use "Phantom Cast" — two words, both capitalized.
  • • Pair the logo with at least 12 px of clear-space.
  • • Use the gradient mark on dark backgrounds; the mono mark on light.
Don't
  • • Don't call it a "deepfake app". It is face-swap software with explicit anti-misuse policy.
  • • Don't recolor the gradient or rotate the iconmark.
  • • Don't use the brand to imply endorsement of synthetic-media use cases we prohibit.
